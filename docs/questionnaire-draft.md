# Creative Provenance Questionnaire — Draft (Revised v3)

10 questions, ~5–7 minutes, anchored to one specific recent piece.

All proportional questions use soft visual weight (drag-to-stack, position on a 2D field, presence/absence) rather than sliders or percentages. The output is a fingerprint, not a measurement.

Voice: warm, evocative, lightly poetic. Plain language over technical terms.

The questionnaire moves through three time horizons: the moment of starting (Q2), the period of making (Q3), and the years of training that shaped how you make (Q4). Each asks about different scales of contribution.
